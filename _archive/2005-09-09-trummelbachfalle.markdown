This rainy afternoon I met up with Dana, and we headed to Trümmelbachfälle
with his friend Judith and her daughter. Trümmelbachfälle is unique in that
the waterfall is inside the mountain, yet you can walk around inside and see
it. The waterfall is glacier fed from the Eiger, Mönch and Jungfrau mountains.

After the waterfalls, Dana and I continued on to the Lauterbrunnen museum.
There were many old tools and photos of people carrying water and hay bails
down the mountain. It is pretty amazing what people are capable of.

Back at the Mountain Hostel, I decided to try something traditional. Cheese
Fondue... little did I know that it contained alcohol. Woo, strong stuff. A
kind gentleman recommended I let the alcohol burn off for a while.

I also met Claire who lives near Manchester, England — she too was travelling
Switzerland with a 22 day Swiss Pass. She informed me that boat routes were
covered with the Swiss Pass, and recommended a hostel in Gryon.

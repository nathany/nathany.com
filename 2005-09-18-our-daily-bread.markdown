Awake at 5 am, crazy. It's a _four hour_ trip to church this morning,
departing at 6:30 from Altnau, and arriving slightly early for the service at
the [International Church of Luzern][1]. The message was on “our daily bread”
— the essentials God provides for this present day. Pastor Ian referenced
Matthew 6 about God feeding the birds, and added that He doesn't put the food
in their mouths. We need to meet God halfway, doing what we can, but
ultimately trusting in Him.

Afterwards, I was offered various brochures of Christian-run hotels in the
Ticano region, access to the Internet and use of the phone to setup my future
accommodations. The pastor and his wife, of New Zealand, both live upstairs
from the church — convenient.

I had previously contacted a Bed & Breakfast in Steinen, and today noticed
that it's quite close to Lucerne. So I arranged to stay one night in the Asia
room, which is a nicely decorated and has its own entrance and ensuite
WC/shower. I really like the sunroof over the bed, but I don't think I'll be
stargazing tonight — much too cloudy. The B&B in Aigle also had one, seems
like a great idea.

Steinen is on the way to Locarno, which is where I'm headed. I've reserved a
place for tomorrow, located right near Lake Maggiore in the town of Ascona.
With everything prepared, and considering how chilly it is today, I am staying
in to rest... tomorrow is a new day.

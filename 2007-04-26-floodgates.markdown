### VoIP much?

Isn’t it fun when acronyms start to be nested in other acronyms? I have had
the pleasure of phoning a number of old friends with Voice over IP (Internet
Protocol) using _Skype Unlimited Calling_. It has worked quite well for the
most part, though some have complained about poor audio quality. Perhaps due
to Internet latency, or perhaps from the battery getting low on my Bluetooth
headset. When it works though, it’s so cool to not worry about long distance
in all of North America.

### Overflowing blessing

My excuse to pick up the phone is that I’m doing “partnership development” for
my summer job with Campus Crusade for Christ. And let me tell you, it has been
an amazing experience. So many people have blessed me with prayers,
encouragement and financial support. In a short two weeks, **more than 75%**
of the funding is already in.

> God has proven that He does “open for you the windows of heaven and pour out
for you a blessing until it overflows.”[^1]

People have been so incredibly generous, and it has been great to catch up
with people.

### Ready, set, hold on a minute

While the remaining funds trickle (or perhaps gush) in, I am busily planning
my trip to Langley, B.C. What a blessing to find accommodations within walking
distance of the office, and with 5 other guys which should prove to be a
blast!

Things are coming together, and I am anxious to get there and get started. For
a number of reasons, my start date has been pushed back to **May the 14th**.
So I still have time…

The other day I was reading the story of Noah. He didn’t depart from the ark
on the first sign, or even when a dove came back with an olive branch. He
waited for the voice of God, which was a month later. It was a good lesson in
patience.

To cover all the costs, I am still holding out for another $900 to come in. To
see my newsletter and further information, please look over the previous
article: _crusader_.

A big thank you to everyone for your support and prayers!


  [^1]: Malachi 3:10
